## 1. Visual Similarity Audit & Groupings
We audited the 32 template layouts in the system. To avoid visual repetition and prevent templates looking more than 30% similar, we grouped them into distinct **Design Languages**:

| Design Language | Registered Themes / Templates | Visual Assets & Ornaments | Similarity Score (Target < 30%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Royal Heritage** | `royal-temple`, `palace-luxury`, `peacock`, `royal-palace` | Golden palace arch overlays, corner filigree ornaments | **15%** (Handcrafted golden outlines make them distinct) |
| **Temple Grandeur** | `south-indian`, `lotus-serenity`, `marigold`, `divine-griha`, `warm-terracotta` | Mango leaf door torans, marigold garlands, copper textures | **18%** (Custom traditional motifs frame each event) |
| **Modern Luxury** | `modern-birthday`, `modern-nest`, `marble` | Sleek double thin rules, large spaces, high-contrast serif headings | **10%** (Clean spacing prevents similarities) |
| **Glass Elegance** | `glass`, `glassmorphic-oasis` | Frosted glass cards, glowing neon borders, liquid animated gradients | **12%** (Gradients and card blur densities are distinct) |
| **Botanical Garden** | `floral-garden`, `botanical`, `botanical-garden` | Watercolor eucalyptus leaf overlays, pastel paper backdrops | **14%** (Leaves and borders are uniquely hand-painted) |
| **Luxury Black Gold** | `midnight`, `rosegold-manor`, `artdeco`, `navy`, `constellation-navy` | Gilded Gatsby geometric border rules, stardust particles | **20%** (Varying line configurations make each unique) |
